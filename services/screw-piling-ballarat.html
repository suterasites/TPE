The ground here

Heavy clay, wet ground, worked history.

Ballarat gives a foundation three things to deal with at once. The ground is largely heavy clay over weathered bedrock and basalt, the region takes more rainfall and colder winters than most of the state so that clay sits saturated for long stretches of the year, and the older suburbs carry the same goldfields legacy of shallow historic workings and backfilled ground that runs through this part of Victoria.

Wet, heavy, reactive clay is difficult ground for anything that relies on an open excavation or a concrete pour. A screw pile avoids both. It is rotated down through the reactive upper zone and anchored in stable material below, with no pit to dig, no water sitting in an open hole, and almost no spoil to cart off a site that is already soft underfoot.

That makes it a practical option through a Ballarat winter as well as a structural one. The install leaves established gardens, paths and driveways largely intact on the older infill blocks, and every pile is driven to the design torque and certified to AS2159 on completion.

Common questions

Screw piling in Ballarat, answered

Can screw piles go in through a Ballarat winter?

The method suits wet ground better than the alternatives, because there is no open excavation to fill with water and no concrete curing in a saturated profile. Site access and the engineer's design still govern what happens on any given job, so send the plans and soil report through and we will quote it against the actual conditions.

Does the heavy clay around Ballarat cause foundation movement?

Reactive clay moves as it takes on and gives up moisture, and that movement is concentrated in the upper active zone. A screw pile is driven through that zone and anchored in stable load-bearing material underneath, so the structure is supported below the layer that is doing the moving.

Do you cover Creswick, Daylesford and Bacchus Marsh?

Yes. The Central Highlands towns sit within the service area, along with Clunes, Ballan and out toward Ararat where the job warrants it. We bring the machine, the pile stock and the certification with us.

What do you need to quote a Ballarat job?

Plans, the engineering, and a soil report if you have one. That gives us pile counts, sizes, depth and connection detail to price properly, and the quote comes back itemised rather than as an estimate. Email them to sales@totalpiling.com.au or call Rob or Tom.
