The ground here

River country ground, engineered properly.

Echuca sits on the Murray floodplain, and the ground shows it. The profile through town and out across the surrounding irrigation country is typically alluvial: sandy loams and silts laid down by the river, sitting over deeper clay, with a water table that runs high close to the river and rises further through wet years. It is soft, variable, water-affected ground, and it is not forgiving of a foundation that was designed on assumption rather than on a soil report.

That profile is exactly where screw piles earn their place. There is no excavation pit to dig and watch fill with water, no concrete sitting in saturated ground, and almost no spoil to cart away. The pile is rotated down through the soft upper material until it reaches the load-bearing depth and design torque your engineer has specified, and the torque reading on each pile tells us what it is actually bearing on rather than what a drawing predicted.

Total Piling works Echuca and the surrounding district off our northern Victorian base, with the Melbourne operation behind it for machinery and pile stock. Riverfront residential, rural and acreage builds, town extensions, sheds and agricultural infrastructure all get the same certified AS2159 install and the same documentation at handover.

Common questions

Screw piling in Echuca, answered

Do you cover Moama and the NSW side of the river?

Yes. Moama sits directly across the Murray from Echuca and we work both banks as one service area. Interstate civil and government work is available more broadly on request when the job scope justifies the travel. Send us the site address with the plans and we will quote it.

Can screw piles handle the high water table around Echuca?

Yes, and this is one of the strongest arguments for them here. A screw pile is driven mechanically rather than poured, so there is no open excavation filling with water and no concrete curing in a saturated profile. The pile passes through the soft, wet upper material and anchors in competent load-bearing soil below, with install torque recorded against the engineer's design.

Which towns around Echuca do you travel to?

Rochester, Kyabram, Tongala, Lockington and Elmore through the Campaspe corridor, and out to Deniliquin, Cohuna and Kerang where the job warrants it. We bring the machine, the pile stock and the certification with us, so a job out of town is run to the same standard as one in town.

Do you work on flood-affected ground?

Foundation design on the northern plains has to account for ground that can sit saturated for extended periods, and that is a soil report and engineering question on every individual site. Send through the soil report and the engineer's design with your plans and we will quote the pile sizing and depth against them.
